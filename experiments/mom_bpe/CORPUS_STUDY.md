### Upstream filtering and limitations

- **Sangraha Verified** uses an IndicLID-prioritized ensemble with CLD3 and NLLB, text cleaning and statistical screens, and MinHash LSH. It brings curated web text, OCR-derived documents and transcripts. Verify complete source-document boundaries, OCR noise, and rights. Exclude Unverified, Synthetic, and confirmed translated or generated material from the primary natural Malayalam experiment. [Sangraha Setu methods](https://arxiv.org/html/2403.06350#S4)
- **FineWeb2** uses Common Crawl, extraction and web filters, GlotLID language/script thresholds, global per-language MinHash clustering, and PII processing. Preserve one representative per duplicate family without upstream cluster upsampling. Crawl coverage and web boilerplate affect domain proportions. Restrict the primary source to `mal_Mlym/train`, excluding Roman-script and removed splits. [FineWeb2 implementation](https://github.com/huggingface/fineweb-2)
- **SMC** uses tree-sitter HTML extraction and sentencex segmentation from a pinned Malayalam Wikipedia dump. Article language is upstream provenance. The card documents neither an independent row-level LID method nor deduplication. Sentence order and article metadata aid review. Tables, headings and citations can survive extraction. Keep fragments grouped by article even when spread across files. [SMC creation recipe](https://huggingface.co/datasets/smcproject/ml-wiki-sentences#dataset-creation)
- **IndicCorpV2** draws heavily on news websites and also includes Wikipedia and OSCAR. Published filtering uses CLD3 and langdetect, script share, short/noisy-content checks and a curated offensive-term list. The card and paper do not establish a usable per-row deduplication ledger for the sampled conversion. Verify duplicates locally and recover original source coordinates before eligibility for a traceable natural-document panel. [IndicCorp data cleaning](https://aclanthology.org/2023.acl-long.693.pdf)
- **HPLT** uses Trafilatura, OpenLID 2.0 and the Monotextor cleaning and global deduplication pipeline for Malayalam. Its public shards are ordered by Web Document Scorer bins and carry predicted registers. Review skew by bin and source, including duplicated material shared with other Common Crawl packages. [HPLT methods](https://hplt-project.org/datasets/v3.0)

Additional leads for the literature stratum are revision-pinned Malayalam Wikisource and Sayahna editions. Both can provide traceable literary source documents. For each work, record the work and edition, author, rights evidence for the applicable jurisdictions, transcriber or editor licenses, and stable book/chapter ancestry. These leads were assessed from publisher documentation and remain outside the measured samples. Whole Malayalam Wikipedia articles remain the encyclopedic lead, separate from SMC fragments. [Malayalam Wikisource licensing](https://ml.wikisource.org/wiki/Wikisource:Copyright_policy) · [Sayahna Digital Collection](https://en.books.sayahna.org/html/en-main.html)

## Bounded sample results

The 332 captured records took 54,757,902 bytes of successful source range transfers excluding metadata, dependencies and failed probes. The verified local captures retain 2,232,489 raw text bytes. Dataset Viewer row endpoints failed, so the survey used pinned Parquet ranges and HPLT compressed prefixes. A stale Sangraha conversion also failed. The successful Sangraha capture used the initial decodable data pages from three native shards, aligned with the original document IDs and source types. FineWeb2 spans four row groups from one usable file, SMC one row group, IndicCorpV2 twelve groups from three partial files, and HPLT the first 14 documents per quality shard. These are limited, structured samples with unequal selection probabilities.

Lexical forms use the configured canonical Malayalam normalization. One occurrence in the sample is a rare-form proxy, with no population-frequency or validity claim. The common 129-token prefix is limited by the entire SMC capture and remains sensitive to ordering and domain. Mlmorph checked a deterministic SHA-rank sample of up to 150 distinct types at most 48 codepoints long. The evidence records Mlmorph 1.4.3, SFST 1.10.0, the reviewed compiled transducer SHA-256, and observed tags for case, tense, voice or other inflectional analyses. Native-speaker adjudication count was zero. Recognition, ambiguity and a broad tag inventory describe analyzer behavior, without demonstrating an alignment or a morphology-aware benefit.

FineWeb2 exposed 54 distinct hosts, HPLT 62 and SMC one encyclopedia host. Sangraha exposed two source types and IndicCorpV2 exposed no domains in these captures. HPLT predicted narrative for 54 rows and additional opinion/information/other registers; these cannot independently fill evaluation domain quotas. No identical or qualifying near-duplicate text links were observed within or across the 332 captured rows. The small sample and common web origins leave substantial overlap risk across FineWeb2, HPLT, Sangraha web and IndicCorpV2. SMC and full Wikipedia require explicit shared article ancestry even for very different-length fragments. IndicCorpV2 includes Wikipedia and OSCAR upstream. Release-level duplication remains unmeasured.

The index groups stable URLs, native source-document IDs, raw hashes, lexical-canonical hashes, and a separate NFC/whitespace view. It verifies near duplicates using exact Jaccard ≥0.85 over sets of character five-grams, with deterministic prefix candidates and an exact length bound. Components are transitive across sources and include quarantined records. Match witnesses are provenance evidence and do not enumerate every exact pair. Templates and shared headers can join unrelated pages, chains can overgroup, and chapter excerpts, paraphrases or rewritten copies can evade Jaccard. Review borderline clusters, book/article/host ancestry, and exposure to downstream proxy corpora before freeze. The split materializer keeps one representative per connected component.

## Source selection and replacement plan

1. Start source permissions work with **Sangraha Verified web** as the core quality candidate and **FineWeb2** as the core provenance candidate. The bounded full-document screening rate was highest for Sangraha, while FineWeb2 gives usable source URLs. Retain 48K for matched primary arms when within-corpus occupancy supports it. Keep one shared manifest-ordered input for A codepoint, B grapheme, and C akshara, with the same specials, normalizer, byte-fallback accounting and no morphology in the first comparison.
2. Use **SMC/full Malayalam Wikipedia** for formal encyclopedic coverage with per-article provenance. Use complete revision-pinned articles for natural-document evaluation. Add independently rights-checked literary sources by work and edition. Introduce **HPLT** as supplementary training only when domain coverage, permissions and deduplicated new vocabulary warrant it. Use **IndicCorpV2** as a training supplement only when ancestry and rights can be recovered. Its legacy-chillu exposure and analyzer coverage make it an informative diagnostic source.
3. Rank more source shards within each reviewed domain by screened, permission-cleared unique bytes, added unique word forms per common byte budget, orthography coverage, and provenance. Record accepted and rejected counts plus uncovered domains. Fill missing domains from specific traceable natural sources. Fix the domain byte caps and source priorities before evaluating any tokenizer. Keep unfilled validation and held-out quotas explicit.
4. The proposed revision retains independent train, validation and held-out partitions. Keep the held-out inputs outside tuning. Use the existing 2,400 natural-document protocol, 400 in each of news, literature, encyclopedic, technical, conversation/regional, and Malayalam–English. Build a separately adjudicated orthography panel of at least 240 cases. A future morphology comparison requires 240 reviewed forms across at least 80 families, independently adjudicated training alignments, and disjoint family assignments. Apply the frozen verifier to a separately assembled manifest with real permissions and separation evidence.
5. The 332-row source-study proposal is an internal allocation and QA artifact. Train/validation/held-out received 269/34/29 rows, of which 231/30/26 passed the provisional screen. None have adjudicated source permissions. The authored 13-record engineering corpus is ready at `runs/corpus-study-v1/authored-diagnostic-reviewed-v1`. Its train/validation/held-out files have 4/5/4 records and 432/863/377 canonical UTF-8 bytes. Its tiny scale supports engineering only. Match every scientific arm's actual canonical exposure and compute downstream BPB as summed predicted bits over shared canonical UTF-8 bytes under the protocol's BOS/EOS policy after a real freeze.

## Reproduction and bounded operation

`corpus_acquire` and `sangraha_pages` are independent source readers. The committed evidence pins the corresponding public storage identities, original file/row coordinates, range hashes and final capture hashes. Run acquisitions into a fresh ignored directory. Check expected bytes and SHA-256 before reusing any result. The receipt records why the Sangraha native-page adapter is separate. It needs PyArrow plus the SHA-pinned Parquet Thrift schema and `thriftpy2`. The survey uses Python 3.12, PyArrow 26.0.0, and the official fastText binary and pinned wrapper in the evidence. The optional Mlmorph run uses the reviewed project transducer and dependency lock. Install sample-only dependencies in an isolated environment.

```powershell
$study = 'experiments/mom_bpe/runs/corpus-study-replay-v1'
$evidence = 'experiments/mom_bpe/corpus-evidence-v1.json'
foreach ($source in @('fineweb2', 'smc', 'indiccorp', 'hplt')) {
    python -m experiments.mom_bpe.corpus_acquire --source $source --evidence $evidence --out "$study/samples"
}
# Put the pinned parquet.thrift and its resources.json together before Sangraha acquisition.
python -m experiments.mom_bpe.sangraha_pages --schema "$study/parquet-tools/parquet.thrift" --out "$study/samples"
# Put the pinned lid.176.ftz beside its resources.json before running the survey.
python -m experiments.mom_bpe.corpus_build --captures "$study/samples" --lid "$study/lid/resources.json" --out "$study/analysis" --morphology
python -m experiments.mom_bpe.corpus_materialize diagnostic --out "$study/authored-diagnostic" --raw-byte-budget 4096
```

For matched development or DGX preparation, pass repeated `--captures` manifests and `--index-only` to `corpus_build`. Supply an immutable ordered inventory of bounded local JSONL shards. Each receipt records `source, shard, file, repo, revision, storage_revision, sha256, bytes, rows`. Each row carries `src,row_index,text,attributes` and may carry `complete_document`. Adapters should retain `source_document_id` and `source_document_url` when verified. Each indexed shard is one SQLite transaction with a verified fingerprint and completion record. Replay resumes from the last transaction. Changes to inputs or the recipe require a new output directory. The default quality, filters, duplicate index, input reader and survey partition function are independently replaceable. Index-only mode bypasses survey-wide vocabulary allocation in RAM.

`corpus_materialize development` and `corpus_materialize dgx` share a disk-backed proposal path. Each takes `--study`, `--review`, `--out`, `--raw-byte-budget` and an optional `--validation-per-domain`. Supply `mom-corpus-permissions-v1` evidence tied to the study recipe SHA-256 with two independent reviewers, reviewed sources and SHA-verified permission files. A source entry supplies its revision, license, attribution, local-use approval and relative training priority. The SHA-verified permission ledger binds every selected record ID to its raw SHA, reviewed domain, eligibility and specific permission scope. Validation and held-out proposals require complete source-traceable documents. Domain packing reserves the held-out quotas first, then validation. A replaceable training byte policy divides the cap across reviewed domains and records its implementation and resolved limits. Whole-document packing can leave bytes unused. Review indexing and assignment use separate durable transaction checkpoints. Staged split files are immutable and verified on replay, and the manifest appears last. The outputs are proposals for the existing scientific gate.

The survey processed 2.23 MB of raw text into roughly 5 MB of SQLite data at about 316 MiB peak process RSS and 30.5 seconds wall time, plus retained inputs and reporting. Start production sizing on a permission-cleared shard pilot. Budget several times the selected raw text size for captures, SQLite postings, WAL, split materializations and verified backup. For example, provision at least 10 GB of scratch for an initial 1 GB selected-raw input capacity check, then resize from measured posting density, WAL and duration. The prefix index can generate expensive candidate sets on repetitive text. Only the bounded CPU survey and authored materialization have workstation-scale evidence. A DGX plan should stream pinned bounded shards to CPU scratch, close each verified transaction, persist permissions and separation receipts, and freeze the exact common input before GPU work is scheduled. GPU availability and physical device selection remain a later experiment step under AGENTS.md.
